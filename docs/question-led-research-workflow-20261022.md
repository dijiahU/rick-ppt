# Question-led content research · 2026-10-22

Version: `0.1.0+codex.20261022questionresearch`.

The product-introduction audit showed that many official sources and a structurally
complete outline could still yield generic feature summaries and delegation advice.
Research needed to explain consequential questions and relationships before deciding
what pages to make. This update changes the existing research/author instructions,
not admission, schemas, review stages or the native authoring architecture.

## Production behavior

- Keep a concise research agenda in the existing source notes, distinct from the
  page outline. Start with audience questions and relevant evidence gaps, refine
  them as evidence arrives, and derive page order from the resulting explanation.
  Supplied outlines, faithful conversions and scoped edits keep their scope.
- Adapt investigation to the subject: disciplinary concepts and worked examples;
  original texts and historical circumstances; actual sites, uses and spatial
  precedents; or product boundaries and firsthand workflows/outputs. These are
  directions to choose from, not required chapters or fixed source/platform lists.
- Investigate mechanisms, conditions and relationships. Follow a useful case through
  its important input/actions/result/limits where supported instead of collecting
  many interchangeable overviews. A case explaining a mechanism does not establish
  its prevalence or general performance.
- Match evidence to claims. Distinguish firsthand reports, inspected outputs,
  acquired assets and reproduced results. Compare relevant perspectives when they
  could change a conclusion; reposts and multiple summaries of one source are not
  independent corroboration. Do not invent inaccessible evidence.
- Synthesize findings, explanation, examples and uncertainty in the same notes.
  Plan their visual expression with the outline; leave exact composition to ordered
  authoring. Text-only accounts can support labeled conceptual illustrations,
  illustrative sequences or editable schematics of supported processes. They must
  not masquerade as actual case evidence or bypass documentary/history constraints.
- Stop initial research when essential questions support a truthful outline and
  further reading repeats known material. Prepare concrete later assets concurrently
  through the existing broker. During authoring, investigate specific new gaps,
  retain valid findings and update affected notes/outline without restarting research.
- Helpers receive different concrete questions with source locations and inspection
  limits; the main author owns synthesis, page order and every native page edit.

Guidance is implemented in production and portable Skill entries, `content.md`,
`research-and-assets.md`, existing branch guidance, shared `runner/workflow.py`
research/author prompts, and `runner/PREFETCH.md`. Local and API paths use those
shared prompts. No new time ratio, source count, image quota, political reading
requirement, mandatory case pattern or extra reviewer was introduced.

The discussion drew methodological inspiration from the original texts

[Preface and Postscript to Rural Surveys](https://www.marxists.org/chinese/maozedong/marxist.org-chinese-mao-194134.htm)
and [On Practice](https://news.12371.cn/2017/08/29/ARTI1504001411770206.shtml).
The PPT workflow applications above are project decisions, not quotations or a
requirement to apply a historical political framework to every subject.

The correction replaces narrowly read subject directions with provisional questions
and broader discovery inside existing `topic_research`. Subject examples are search
starting points, not domain/source whitelists or an exhaustive taxonomy. Relevant
creator experiences, outputs, works, debates and cross-field connections can change
the questions and outline. Discovery can use secondary leads and social sources;
selected factual claims still need appropriate original evidence. User accounts
are evidence for reported experience rather than guarantees.

Audience value and explanatory fit accompany credibility when selecting material.
Repeated official overviews do not establish that a whole subject has been explored:
change vocabulary, angle or community when a useful question/visual need remains.

was introduced. Supplied-only work, targeted verification and scoped edits keep
their source and scope constraints. Concurrent preparation follows the same guidance.
