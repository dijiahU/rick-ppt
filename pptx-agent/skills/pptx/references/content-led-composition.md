# Sequential design and native craft

One continuing designer owns the deck and its scoped repairs, in presentation order.
Read [Art direction](art-direction.md) once. [Worked decisions](art-direction-examples.md)
show useful operations and rendered checks, not layouts, a reference quota or task
answers; the host supplies them at first author entry when available, and CLI-only
harnesses can read a relevant example directly.

## Establish the deck's visual agreement

Read the brief, ordered outline, actual template and source material, keeping
source/user constraints apart from suggested treatments. In design_direction/source
notes set a short provisional agreement: the subject's visual character,
typography/color roles, relationship/annotation grammar and how the sequence serves
the audience. A blank branded body does not prescribe its composition. Scope, meaning,
editability, fonts and native behavior are protected; density, scale, asymmetry,
layering and image/type treatment are open. Test the agreement on the first
substantive page in normal order (an attractive title does not validate the body);
update a failed premise before propagating, keeping effective pages and encoding.

## Resolve the current page before detailing it

A decision order inside ordinary authoring — not five model stages or a written form.
Keep only consequential decisions in existing notes.

1. **Audience task.** What this audience must understand, compare, judge, notice or
   experience here, with its conditions, connected to the preceding explanation; a
   topic heading is not enough.
2. **Concrete subject and evidence.** Choose what carries the task: an actual
   work/image, source passage, data, code/rule fragment, state change or native
   relationship, and inspect the relevant original detail. The research summary is
   supported narrative, not paragraphs to distribute among containers. Keep facts,
   units, source identity and qualifications; separate explanatory illustration from
   real case output. For a source visual choose original display, a detail crop with
   editable annotation, or reconstruction, weighing what the audience must see
   against what the user must edit — editable slides do not require every image to
   become shapes, though explicit all-native/fidelity requirements win. A case
   explaining an operation needs the detail that exposes its effect (input and changed
   state, an exact rule acting on an example, an inspectable output fragment); a
   component name or benefit statement only orients. Use supported evidence or a
   clearly identified permitted illustration — no invented real screenshot/result,
   no compulsory example on unrelated pages.
3. **Expression and composition.** Before coordinates, separate the relationship from
   its component inventory: in a process the acted-on object, operation and
   consequence (context apart from the execution path); in a comparison what is shared
   and what differs. Attach numbers, conditions and annotations to what they qualify,
   then decide how attention follows the relationship: focal scale, hierarchy, space
   and image/type relations in the deck's visual world. When uncertain, contrast
   plausible expressions briefly and develop one; a worked decision or inspected work
   can settle a specific problem, layout names and palette values cannot. Moving from
   mechanism to case, keep entities, branches and comparison conditions recognizable
   (read the saved preceding slide if needed) rather than flattening to a cleaner
   list. A source image carrying the explanation is cropped and scaled for inspection
   with enough labels/context; an optional overview may stay small when the essential
   explanation is visible elsewhere.
4. **Main construction and rendered test.** Build subject, relationships and key
   wording with actual material before decoration. Plan entry context, semantic click
   groups, persistent orientation and the complete final state together; related
   labels/connectors enter together and groups need no visible card. Render when the
   expression can be judged and receive the actual preview through the image channel —
   a path/render report is not a look at the page.
5. **Finish and retain.** Once the main expression works, refine spacing, alignment,
   annotations, compatible text/connectors and semantic reveals. Finish the subject's
   typography: meaningful emphasis, intentional breaks, mathematical indices/fractions,
   decisive code/data fields; keep literal source code. Use technical/formula-fidelity.md
   for notation; formula_inventory entries are encoding clues, not an aesthetic or math
   verdict. Plan nodes and connection space together and check label ownership,
   baselines of comparable rows, branch spacing and line/text separation in the render.
   Ordinary process/branch arrows prefer horizontal/vertical runs with consistent
   visible weight (about 2–2.5 pt on a standard 16:9 slide; judge the render) — no
   thin diagonal fans between misaligned boxes. Keep necessary spatial/directional
   geometry and faithful-source constraints; endpoint correctness is not good routing.
   Use measured text objects or native cells for columns, not repeated spaces. A strong
   simple page can stand. Checkpoint a complete candidate after a meaningful finished
   correction, before optional polish.

The order guides decisions, not appearance: comparable data may share a quiet
table/plot, original words may lead a text page, a mechanism can show changing states.
No image, layout diversity, artist, complexity or revision quota applies.

## Judge the expression in the existing render loop

Judge the page's audience task at reading scale with relevant supplied references:
trace a diagram's dependencies, locate the observations supporting an evidence claim,
read a text-led page's hierarchy. Can the viewer inspect the useful detail and follow
the relation? Point to where the title's promised explanation happens. Judge visual
construction separately from logic — a traceable graph can still route awkwardly and
lack hierarchy. If the main expression fails, change subject construction, scale or
composition before borders, and do not keep a weak arrangement because it matches
earlier colors. Missing/unsuitable material can justify focused permitted acquisition
or another provisional expression; never invent facts/screens or silently replace
mandatory supplied material.

For a consequential change keep the previous render and a native snapshot, then edit,
render/view and compare the affected region (page context compare_previous supplies
historical pixels; otherwise open the saved images). Read delivered images directly;
reopen only for missing input or detail. No decorative changes to prove effort.
Author judgment within time bounds is not independent approval or a design score. At
section boundaries compare existing previews for progression, identity and rhythm,
without re-rendering unchanged pages or a per-page checklist.

## Save a complete candidate

Use native helpers or direct OOXML for the exact chosen objects (helpers select no
layout); measure genuine fonts and keep original parts/IDs/relationships in scoped
edits. When the deck is complete, scripts/candidate_save.py (or manifest
save_candidate) exports, renders natively, runs the authored decision check when
records exist and atomically declares delivery.json; use a fresh name. Save the main
finished repair before extra polish and refresh after further completed corrections. A
failed save keeps the prior declaration; retained working edits are not a deliverable.
Independent content/visual reviews and exact frozen-artifact delivery remain required;
static rendering and timing checks do not certify target-player playback.
