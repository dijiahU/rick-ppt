# Research, visual references and assets

For factual research use content.md: discover broadly when appropriate, inspect
actual sources, verify selected claims and distinguish fact, reported experience,
interpretation and illustration. Search snippets are leads. Keep source locations,
conditions and consequential uncertainty in source-notes.md. Never send private
attachments or credentials into a public query. Honor offline/supplied-only scope.

## Design exploration

For new decks or substantive redesigns with creative latitude, actively consider
references beyond the supplied frame. Inspect relevant original design work, art,
photography, scientific illustration, film, architecture or teaching when it can
strengthen the visual direction. Choose by craft and task fit, not fame or awards.
An institutional template and a specific paper do not close this exploration:
preserve their actual protected elements and factual meaning while designing the body.
Source-limited briefs and faithful/scoped edits retain their requested boundaries.

Search for the subject and the visual question together. Follow promising visual
leads rather than a fixed artist roster. Actual compositions/frames are needed to
learn composition; attribution and descriptions alone are insufficient. Separate
creator intent from your interpretation. Transfer a useful relationship into the
current subject and inspect its effect in the render. Reuse effective findings;
search again for a materially different need. Stop once an approach can be tried.
No required artist/reference count, per-page search or new approval stage.

Register inspected work, observations and concrete page actions once using
workflow-branches.md. Tools derive page associations and slide notes. A reference
record does not prove good design; the adaptation must be useful on the actual page.
Inspiration study and acquiring rights/bytes for embedding are separate activities.

## Choose material for the explanation

Photographs, authentic interfaces, native diagrams, charts, designed text and original
illustrations can combine. Make useful subjects, outputs and changes visible;
choose imagery when it improves this content rather than to fill space. Exact
wording or analytical relationships may appropriately lead a page without a picture.

Historical/documentary people, events and archival objects require authentic
material. Real app screens and case evidence cannot be fabricated. When optional
real imagery is unavailable, sourced text or an editable analytical diagram is valid.
An explicitly required missing source remains a limitation. A text-only account may
support a labeled conceptual illustration/native example of its supported process,
without claiming it is an observed output or independently reproduced success.
Generation is available for suitable original concepts/scenes/proposed effects,
including as a first choice, with an illustrative label; failed search alone does
not determine that choice. Inspect the result and keep essential analysis editable.

Follow existing task-scoped brokers/paths for acquisition. For a failed URL or
non-media response, inspect the actual error and use a legitimate accessible source
or revise the expression. Transport recovery is bounded; avoid blind URL swaps.
Partial/oversized files may need a smaller source or documented conversion preserving
meaning. Update the asset record and report unmet requirements honestly.

## Choose assets for their explanatory job

### Prepare ahead without splitting authorship

One main author maintains the deck's narrative, visual direction and sequential
native edits. Independent searches, source inspection and candidate asset work
can overlap current-page composition when the host exposes asynchronous helpers.
After resolving evidence essential to planning, submit concrete later-page needs
in a batch and continue authoring; planning may retain the existing planned
asset status until acquisition. Choose lookahead by dependencies, without a
fixed page count or search quota. Check several results near their point of use;
never busy-poll or wait for every later asset before starting the deck.

Give each request stable page IDs, its specific question/purpose, source limits
and the needed asset role. Helpers do not set the page's final composition or edit
the deck, outline or author notes. The main author inspects returned evidence and
actual files, chooses them, records provenance/application and updates the plan.
Prepared is not accepted or visually inspected by the author. Preserve actual
inspection limits, generated/documentary distinctions, existing acquisition
limits and failure routes. Reuse valid completed work on resume; changed relevant
inputs need a fresh decision, not automatic regeneration. Ordinary chat is not a
content revision. When no async helper exists, batch independent available tool
calls without inventing a background capability.

- When a chosen image establishes a real identity/place/event, inspect its provenance and context. Generated imagery is illustrative, never documentary evidence.
- When choosing a scientific/mechanical visual, ground its geometry, relationships and labels in authoritative evidence; simplification must preserve the mechanism.
- For quantitative comparisons, select a representation that supports the actual comparison and required editability, with units and traceable data; no automatic chart/table choice.
- For original atmosphere or concepts, choose sourced imagery, generation or native art by task fit. Generate focused raster assets when that choice helps, preserving required editable content.
- When meaning depends on motion, inspect suitable media or supported native states; a poster frame does not verify motion.

Inspect the actual image at its intended size, including resolution, crop, contrast, labels and language. A search thumbnail is not sufficient. Keep editable slide copy separate from raster artwork. Search for a better asset or change the composition when the first candidate does not explain the point; no image quota or fixed retry count.

For reused media record creator, source page, asset URL, reuse/embedding basis and required attribution. Do not remove watermarks, evade access controls or assume viewing access permits redistribution. Use supplied media or a suitable alternative if permission or acquisition cannot be established. Preserve attribution in notes or a source line as appropriate.

## Work within real capabilities

Check the host tools/capabilities. Hosted search and image generation are separate from shell network access. A search result URL is not a locally embedded asset. Import only through permitted task-scoped paths/brokers; never widen access to personal asset directories or silently switch to paid APIs. Inspect generated assets for incorrect structures, unintended text and visual defects; record prompt, file and role in source-notes.md. Follow the available image-generation skill when applicable.

For embedding, read images.md and relationships.md; for charts read charts.md. Use animations.md for native reveal timing and actual host instructions for video/audio. Verify relevant frames, semantic order, start behavior, pace, cropping and target-player playback when available. Report separately which checks were possible. Static rendering cannot certify animation, GIF or video playback. If verified motion is essential and no player exists, report the unresolved requirement.

Record consequential defects and corrections in review.md through the separate content-review.md and visual-review.md workflows. Do not mistake technical export success for audience comprehension or visual quality.
